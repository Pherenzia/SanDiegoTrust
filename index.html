Our Services

We provide personalized, attentive support to help make your life in San Diego safe, comfortable, and fulfilling—support that is only possible because we are here locally.

Finding Your Home

With rising rents and stricter rental conditions, it has become challenging for Japanese residents to find housing on their own in San Diego. We'll support you every step of the way, using our trusted local network to help you find the perfect homestay or room rental that fits your needs.

Community College

Some community colleges accept English proficiency scores such as Eiken or Duolingo, making admission easier compared to four-year universities. Tuition is also significantly more affordable. One of the main advantages is that students can improve their English skills while taking courses in their field of interest. After completing two years of study, it's possible to transfer to a four-year university, such as the University of California or California State University. From school selection and application procedures to local support after arrival, we provide comprehensive assistance every step of the way.

Language Schools

From university-affiliated language programs to well-known international schools recognized in Japan, as well as affordable and friendly local schools that can only be found here in San Diego, we offer a wide range of options. Based on your goals and preferences, we'll recommend the school that best fits your needs and support you throughout the entire enrollment process.

Student Visa Application Support

If you plan to study in the U.S. for more than 90 days, you'll need to obtain an F-1 student visa. We handle all the complicated steps for you—from receiving your school's admission document (Form I-20) and paying the SEVIS fee, to completing the DS-160 visa application form and scheduling your embassy interview. Our goal is to make sure even first-time applicants can prepare for studying abroad with confidence, through careful and reliable support every step of the way.

Kids' Camps

We offer a wide variety of camps during spring, summer, and winter breaks, where children can not only learn English but also experience exciting programs in sports, art, science, and more. We'll help you choose the best camp based on your child's age and interests, and provide full support from registration to participation. Even if it's their first time abroad, you can feel confident knowing we provide attentive, local support to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

Consultation & Support

We're available for questions or consultations not only by email, but also through video calls on Zoom or LINE. If any issues or troubles arise during your stay, our responsive support system allows us to assist you quickly and reliably, helping ease your concerns and provide peace of mind every step of the way.

About the Founder

Kazumi Riley

Founder / Local Support Advisor

I have lived in San Diego for more than 20 years and in the United States for over 26 years.

I came to the United States from Japan 26 years ago. Like many people starting a new life in another country, I experienced many challenges as I adjusted to a different language, culture, and way of life.

Those experiences have shaped the way I approach my work today. I want people who are beginning a new chapter of their lives in San Diego to feel as little stress and uncertainty as possible and to have a safe and comfortable start to their new life.

Over the years, I have had the opportunity to support many international students, corporate employees and their families, as well as people of all ages—from young children to seniors.

Living abroad can be an exciting and rewarding experience, filled with new opportunities, friendships, and challenges. At the same time, adjusting to a new language and culture, finding housing and schools, handling everyday procedures, and navigating unfamiliar situations can sometimes feel overwhelming.

With more than 20 years of experience living and working in San Diego, I have built a strong local network and developed a deep understanding of the community. I provide personalized support based on each individual's needs, from finding housing and connecting with schools to assisting with everyday life, communicating with local organizations, and responding to unexpected situations when help is needed.

Having someone you can trust locally can make a tremendous difference when living abroad.

Because we are based here in San Diego, we are able to provide the prompt, flexible, and personal support that can be difficult to receive from overseas. Through the “local support that only someone on the ground can provide,” we are committed to helping each person feel secure, confident, and comfortable as they begin their new life in San Diego.

Knowing that there is someone you can turn to when you need help.

Knowing that there is someone you can rely on locally.

That sense of security is what we strive to provide.
